## Rules

Important comparison and novelty papers must be verified from original or authoritative sources.

Preferred sources:

1. ACL Anthology;
2. official proceedings/publisher;
3. arXiv;
4. reliable scholarly metadata services.

No unverified BibTeX should enter the final manuscript.
