# Claim-Evidence Ledger

This ledger tracks manuscript claims while research continues.

| ID | Claim | Evidence | Class | Scope | Status | Allowed wording | Paper locations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

`SUPPORTED`
→ may be written within scope.

`PARTIAL`
→ must use bounded wording.

`PROVISIONAL`
→ research hypothesis only.

`GAP`
→ evidence required before claim.

`REJECTED`
→ cannot appear as positive contribution.

Do not copy a research hypothesis into the Abstract or Contributions until its status is promoted.
