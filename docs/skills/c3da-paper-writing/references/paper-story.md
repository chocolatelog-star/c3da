# Paper Story

## Core rule

The manuscript is not a chronological list of experiments.

The final paper should communicate one scientific argument:

```text
problem
→ verified limitation
→ technical hypothesis
→ final surviving method
→ controlled evidence
→ mechanism analysis
→ limitations
```

## During ongoing research

Do not prematurely freeze the final story.

Maintain:

- stable motivation;
- provisional research questions;
- candidate contributions;
- unresolved evidence.

When a candidate fails, revise the story instead of hiding the failure.

## Contribution test

Every final contribution must answer:

1. What is technically new?
2. Why is it needed?
3. Where does it act in the pipeline?
4. What evidence supports it?
5. What is the scope of the evidence?
6. What remains unresolved?

If a claimed contribution has no accepted evidence source, it is not yet a final contribution.
